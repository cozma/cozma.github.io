Radio · Monthly

Nicegroove on Eaton Radio

Dag hosts Nicegroove, a monthly radio show on Eaton Radio, the community radio station broadcasting out of the Eaton Hotel in Washington, D.C. Each episode brings in guest DJs from the D.C. underground dance scene, often going back to back, alongside live performances, live painting and even an all-vinyl session. The sound centers on house, tech house, deep house, afro house and techno, with detours into amapiano, Latin house, disco, R&B and hip hop.
