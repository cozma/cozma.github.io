Dag Yeshiwas

Engineer · Artist · Musician · Host

By day, Dag is a software engineer with 10+ years of full-stack experience. He is currently a Principal Software Engineer at Capital One, and has previously worked at College Board, SoundExchange, WireWheel and Mapbox. He studied Computer Science and Human-Computer Interaction at Virginia Tech. He also makes music as dagher, and DJs bars, parties and events throughout Washington, D.C.

He builds interactive sound installations that let people play with things that usually stay silent: the electrical signals of living plants, or the space between a visitor and a pillar of light. He has worked with the Smithsonian’s Hirshhorn Museum on several of them (see the projects).
